Privacy

For azizcanyalcin.github.io and its game, the Realm of Azizcan. Last updated 1 October 2026.

The short version

There are no accounts, ads or trackers here. Your game progress stays in your own browser. Apart from a bot check when the game's page opens, the only data that leaves your device is your talks with the tavern's two voice characters, Od-Man and Gēhān, if you choose to talk by voice.

Talking to Od-Man and Gēhān

Both are AI voices. When you speak with either by voice:

  • Your voice is streamed to the voice service the keepers run on, which turns speech into text and their replies into speech. The audio is never recorded: no recording of you, or of them, is kept.
  • A transcript of the talk, and anything you tell him (for example the name, craft and guild he asks for, which you are free to leave out), is kept by the voice service for 7 days and then deleted. It is processed by the language model that gives the keepers their words.
  • What they remember of you (the name, craft and guild you gave, the earlier roads you told of, their short notes about past talks, and a short word one may leave for the other) is saved in your browser. It is shared by both keepers and sent along at the start of each talk with either of them, so each talk's 7-day transcript includes it.
  • A bot check. When the game's page opens, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you are a person, so a talk can begin without waiting. When you do talk, a small Cloudflare service counts talks so no one can use up his voice for everyone. It stores a hashed form of your IP address, never the address itself, and forgets it after 48 hours.

Entering the realm asks for your microphone. If you would rather not use voice, deny it: they then speak with you in text, and nothing you say is sent anywhere.

Your game progress

Progress, what you told the keepers and their memory of you are saved in your browser (local storage), and sent only as described above. The menu's Reset Progress deletes all of it.

Fonts and code from other hosts

The pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, and the voice code from jsDelivr when the game's page opens, so a talk can begin without waiting. Both see your IP address when the files are fetched.

The people in the game

The figures in the realm are fictional. The AI voice speaks for no one.

Questions or deletion

To ask about your data or have a transcript deleted before the 7 days are up, write to me on LinkedIn.
